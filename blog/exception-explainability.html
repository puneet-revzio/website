Introducing exception explainability

Raw variances are not actionable. revzio turns breaks into summaries, likely causes, and next steps your team can use immediately.

Read article

April 18, 2026 · Revzio Editorial · 8 min read

The problem with raw exceptions

A $42,000 variance with no narrative forces your team into detective mode. Export two systems, VLOOKUP until something looks familiar, ping three Slack channels, and hope the owner still remembers the deal. That loop is why closes slip — not because accountants lack skill, but because the system hands them noise instead of work.

Raw exceptions also age badly. A timing difference that was obvious on Tuesday is opaque by the following Monday. Continuous close only works if breaks arrive with enough context to act the same day.

What explainability means in revzio

Explainability layers context onto every break: a short summary, likely causes (timing, FX, partial period, mapping change, duplicate), suggested checks, and links to supporting transactions. Reviewers approve or override in product — the trail stays intact for audit.

This is not a black-box “AI said so.” Controllers still set match policy and materiality. The agent prepares the investigation; humans keep judgment. That model is the same human-in-the-loop approach across Orion.

How it shows up in the workflow

Inside the Reconciliation Agent, unmatched or tolerance-break items land in an exception queue with ownership and due dates. From there, reviewers can:

  • Accept the suggested resolution when evidence matches policy
  • Override with a reason code that becomes part of the immutable log
  • Escalate when the break implies a process or data issue upstream

Those decisions feed close workflow status so controllers see whether recon is truly ready — not just “mostly tied.”

Why this shortens close

Teams that resolve exceptions mid-month stop discovering them on day five. Deferred revenue, payout batches, and multi-entity FX stop being fire drills. Pair explainability with the Reconciliation Agent setup guide and the 3-day close guide to move prep into every day of the cycle.

What to look for in a demo

Ask to see a real break: summary text, linked source lines, and the approval trail after an override. Compare that to a CSV of unmatched amounts. If your auditors would rather sample the former, you are looking at the right layer of product depth — also covered in our reconciliation deep dive and reconciliation product page.
