Cutting close by three days at scale

How a Series C finance team reduced close duration with continuous reconciliation and in-product sign-offs.

Read article

April 10, 2026 · Revzio Editorial · 7 min read

Starting point

A 200-person SaaS company was closing in nine business days with three full-time analysts on reconciliation alone. Spreadsheets were the system of record for matching.

What changed

They moved Stripe–NetSuite matching to continuous runs, standardized checklists per entity, and shifted sign-offs into revzio. Analyst time moved from data gathering to review.

Results

Close dropped to six days within two cycles. Exception volume fell 40% because issues surfaced mid-month. External audit sample testing shortened materially.
