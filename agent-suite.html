Orion Agents

Purpose-built for every close workflow

Seven specialized agents. Human-in-the-loop. Audit-ready from day one.

  1. 01

    Reconciliation agent

    Our reconciliation agent runs continuously across bank accounts, payment processors, billing systems, and ERP — matching transactions in real time, handling exceptions with context, and keeping your books audit-ready before month-end arrives.

  2. 02

    Journal entry agent

    The agent pulls data from your bank, payroll, billing, and ERP systems to generate journal entries automatically — with no-code logic, built-in validations, and a complete evidence trail that satisfies any auditor.

  3. 03

    Accounts payable agent

    Our accounts payable agent fetches invoices from any source, extracts line-item data with AI vision, performs 3-way match automatically, and routes payments for approval — so your team handles exceptions and relationships, not data entry.

  4. 04

    Accounts receivable agent

    Our agent automates cash application, ranks your receivables by collection priority, and triggers follow-up sequences automatically — so your team recovers revenue faster without the manual grind.

  5. 05

    Accruals agent

    Orion Accruals agent calculates prepaid amortization, accrual postings, and period-end adjustments from source data — and posts them to your ERP automatically, with a complete schedule every auditor can follow.

  6. 06

    Revenue validation agent

    Validate every revenue event against ASC 606, flag billing-ERP gaps before they hit audit.

  7. 07

    Close management agent

    Our Close Management agent orchestrates your entire month-end close — checklists, owners, dependencies, approvals, and variance summaries — so nothing gets missed and your team knows exactly where they stand at every point in the cycle.
