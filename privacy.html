How revzio collects, uses, and protects your information.

Overview

Revzio Pte. Ltd. ("Revzio," "we," "us," "our") is a company incorporated in Singapore. We provide reconciliation, accounts payable, and financial close software for finance teams, delivered through our website at www.revzio.ai and our application at app.revzio.ai (together, the "Service").

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. It applies to visitors to our website, users of our application, and anyone who connects a third-party account — including a Google account — to Revzio.

If you use Revzio through an employer or organisation, that organisation is the controller of the data in its workspace, and we process that data as its service provider under our Data Processing Addendum.

Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account and contact details — name, work email address, company name, job title, and phone number when you register, request a demo, or contact support.
  • Billing information — company billing address and subscription details. Card payments are handled by our payment processor; we do not store full card numbers.
  • Support communications — messages, attachments, and screenshots you send us.

Information from systems you connect

Revzio only reads from a third-party system after you explicitly connect it and authorise the relevant permissions. Depending on what you connect, this may include:

  • Accounting and ERP systems (for example NetSuite, QuickBooks) — ledgers, invoices, bills, journal entries, vendor and customer records.
  • Payment and banking systems (for example Stripe) — transactions, payouts, settlement and statement data.
  • Google Workspace / Gmail — see Google user data below, which describes this in detail.

Information we collect automatically

  • Usage data — features used, pages viewed, actions taken in the application, and timestamps.
  • Technical data — IP address, browser type, device and operating system, and referring URL.
  • Cookies — we use strictly necessary cookies to keep you signed in and secure the Service, and analytics cookies to understand product usage. You can control non-essential cookies through your browser settings.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use personal information for behavioural advertising.

Google user data

Revzio's application at app.revzio.ai offers an optional Gmail connection so that finance documents arriving in your mailbox can be pulled into your reconciliation workspace without manual downloading and re-uploading. This section describes exactly what we access, why, and what we do with it. The Gmail connection is entirely optional — Revzio works without it.

Scopes we request and why

When you connect Gmail, Google shows you a consent screen listing the permissions below. You choose whether to grant them.

ScopeWhat it allowsWhy Revzio needs it
gmail.readonly Read messages, attachments, and labels. Revzio cannot delete, alter, or send mail with this permission. To identify emails carrying finance documents and extract supplier invoices, bills, and receipts — including PDF and image attachments — into your Revzio workspace.
gmail.send Send email on your behalf from your connected address. To send vendor follow-ups that you initiate from within Revzio — for example, requesting a missing or corrected invoice. Revzio never sends email automatically without an action by you.

How we use Google user data

We use data obtained through Google APIs for one purpose: to provide the document-ingestion and vendor-communication features you see in the Revzio application. Specifically, we:

  • Identify emails carrying invoices, bills, receipts, and related finance attachments.
  • Extract those attachments and the accompanying invoice details — such as vendor, invoice number, date, amount, currency, and line items — using automated processing, including AI-assisted extraction, so the document can be matched against your ledger.
  • Store the extracted document and its fields in your Revzio workspace so your team can review, approve, and reconcile them, and so they remain available as audit evidence.
  • Send vendor follow-up emails that you compose or approve in the application.

We do not use Google user data for advertising, for credit or lending decisions, or for any purpose unrelated to the features described above.

How we store and protect Google user data

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens are encrypted at rest and are never exposed in the application interface or logs.
  • Message content and attachments retrieved from Gmail are encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256), and are isolated per customer workspace.
  • Access is limited to the members of your workspace whom your administrator has authorised.
  • We retrieve only what the features require. Messages that are not identified as finance documents are not stored — only the metadata needed to skip them on subsequent scans.

How we share Google user data

We do not sell Google user data and we do not transfer it to third parties for their own purposes. We share it only with the infrastructure subprocessors that operate the Service on our behalf — cloud hosting, storage, and document-extraction providers — under written contracts that restrict them to processing on our instructions. A current subprocessor list is available on request at contact@revzio.ai. We may also disclose data if legally compelled, as described under Sharing and disclosure.

No Revzio employee reads the contents of your Gmail messages, except in the narrow circumstances permitted by Google's policy: where you have given explicit consent for specific messages (for example, to debug a support issue you have raised), where it is necessary for security or abuse investigations, or where required by law.

Artificial intelligence and machine learning

Revzio's Orion agents use AI models to read finance documents and extract structured fields. Where Google user data is processed this way, it is processed solely to produce results for your own workspace.

We do not use Google Workspace data to develop, improve, or train generalised artificial intelligence or machine learning models, whether our own or a third party's. Where a third-party model provider is used for extraction, it is contractually bound not to train on or retain your data.

Limited Use disclosure

Revzio's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Our use of information received from Google Workspace APIs also adheres to the Google Workspace API User Data and Developer Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention, revocation, and deletion

You can disconnect Gmail from Revzio at any time, either in the application's integration settings or directly in your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access immediately stops all further data retrieval.

When you disconnect, we delete the stored OAuth tokens and any cached message metadata within 30 days. Documents and extracted records already posted into your workspace are retained as part of your accounting records — because they form part of your audit trail — until you or your administrator deletes them, or until your account is closed. To request deletion of all Google-derived data, email contact@revzio.ai and we will action it within 30 days.

How we use information generally

  • To provide, operate, maintain, and improve the Service.
  • To authenticate users and secure accounts.
  • To respond to support requests and communicate about the Service, including operational notices.
  • To send product and marketing communications where you have consented or where permitted by law. You can opt out at any time.
  • To detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents.
  • To comply with legal, accounting, and regulatory obligations.

Legal bases for processing

Where the EU or UK GDPR applies, we process personal data on the basis of: performance of a contract with you; our legitimate interests in operating and securing the Service; your consent (for marketing and for optional integrations such as Gmail); and compliance with legal obligations. Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it at any time.

Sharing and disclosure

We share personal data with:

  • Subprocessors that host, store, monitor, or support the Service, under contractual confidentiality and security obligations.
  • Professional advisers such as auditors and legal counsel, where necessary and under a duty of confidence.
  • Authorities or other parties where required by law, court order, or valid regulatory request, or to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.
  • An acquirer in the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets — in which case we will notify affected customers and the data remains subject to this policy until superseded.

We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

International transfers

Revzio is incorporated in Singapore, operates an office in India, and uses cloud infrastructure that may process data in other jurisdictions. Our personnel in India may access personal data to provide support, operate, and maintain the Service, subject to the same access controls and confidentiality obligations as the rest of our team.

Where personal data is transferred out of the EEA, the UK, or Singapore, we rely on appropriate safeguards — including the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and equivalent contractual protections consistent with the Singapore Personal Data Protection Act 2012 ("PDPA") transfer limitation obligation.

Data retention

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy. Account and workspace data is retained for the life of your subscription and deleted within 90 days of account closure, unless a longer period is required for legal, tax, or audit purposes. Google-derived data follows the shorter timelines described above.

Security

We maintain administrative, technical, and physical safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the data we handle, including encryption in transit and at rest, role-based access control, least-privilege internal access, audit logging, secure software development practices, and regular security testing. More detail is available on our Security and Trust center pages. No system is perfectly secure, but we work continuously to protect your data and will notify you and any relevant regulator of a qualifying breach without undue delay.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, correct inaccuracies, request deletion, restrict or object to certain processing, receive a portable copy, and withdraw consent. Under Singapore's PDPA you may request access to and correction of your personal data, and withdraw consent to its collection, use, or disclosure. Under the GDPR and California's CCPA/CPRA you have the additional rights described in those laws, including the right not to be discriminated against for exercising them.

To exercise any of these rights, email contact@revzio.ai. We will respond within the period required by applicable law. If you are an end user of a customer's workspace, we will refer your request to that customer, who controls the data. EEA and UK residents also have the right to complain to their local supervisory authority.

Children

Revzio is a business product and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the Service or the law. We will post the revised version here with a new "last updated" date, and for material changes we will notify customers by email or in-product notice before the change takes effect.
