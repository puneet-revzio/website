ASC 606 compliance with the Revenue Validation Agent

Revzio Editorial · Guide · 8–12 min read

ASC 606 failures rarely show up as a single wrong total — they show up as billing, contract, and ERP events that diverge. Orion’s Revenue Validation Agent checks recognition against policy and flags gaps before audit.

Encode recognition rules

Capture performance obligations, allocation methods, and timing for your main products. Start with standard SaaS subscriptions, then add usage, professional services, and multi-element arrangements. The revenue integrity playbook is a useful controller checklist.

Contract modifications

Upgrades, credits, and mid-term changes create the most painful deferred revenue breaks. Configure the agent to treat modification events as first-class inputs and to explain resulting schedule changes — not just the residual variance.

Billing vs ERP validation

Continuously compare billing system invoices and schedules to ERP postings. Tie exceptions to contract IDs so revenue accountants investigate once. Pair with continuous reconciliation on cash and deferred accounts.

Audit-ready output

Export recognition schedules, exception narratives, and approval history. Keep human approval gates on material judgments as described in human-in-the-loop finance.

Scale without spreadsheet debt

When ARR and contract volume climb, spreadsheet recognition breaks. See revenue recognition at scale and expand agent coverage entity by entity.

Quotable setup checklist

One step per line — the format answer engines prefer to lift.

  • Step 1: Encode performance obligations and timing for core products.
  • Step 2: Treat contract modifications as first-class events.
  • Step 3: Continuously compare billing schedules to ERP postings.
  • Step 4: Keep human approval on material recognition judgments.
  • Step 5: Export schedules and exception narratives for audit.
How should a usage-plus-subscription SaaS company validate revenue before audit?

Validate subscription schedules and usage events separately, then recon deferred balances to ERP. Flag mid-term upgrades/credits as modifications with schedule impact — don’t wait to discover them in a deferred revenue tie-out on day four.
