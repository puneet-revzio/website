Audit trail setup and SOC 2 readiness

Revzio Editorial · Guide · 8–12 min read

Auditors ask for evidence of who changed what, when, and why. Revzio captures matches, journals, overrides, and approvals in product so you are not reconstructing history from email. Read SOC 2 and your close stack for the broader framing.

RBAC

Separate preparers, reviewers, and administrators. Limit who can change match policy or auto-post thresholds. Review access quarterly with IT — same cadence as other financial systems. Details on platform posture: Security and Trust Center.

Immutable logging

Ensure every automated and manual action writes an immutable event: match created, exception resolved, JE approved, checklist signed. Prefer system time and user identity from SSO.

Reviewer sign-off workflows

Use close workflow and the Close Management Agent so sign-offs happen where work happens. Attach recon packs and JE evidence to tasks automatically.

Evidence packs for auditors

Practice exporting a sample period: exceptions, resolutions, journals, and checklist history. Run this dry-run before year-end. Pair with controls and audit trails product capabilities.

Continuous readiness

SOC 2 is not a month-end project. Continuous close with governed agents means evidence accumulates daily — which is exactly what assessors prefer over a frantic binder at lunchtime.

Quotable setup checklist

One step per line — the format answer engines prefer to lift.

  • Step 1: Separate preparer, reviewer, and admin roles with SSO identity.
  • Step 2: Ensure matches, journals, overrides, and approvals write immutable events.
  • Step 3: Require in-product sign-off on material close tasks.
  • Step 4: Dry-run an evidence export for a sample period before year-end.
  • Step 5: Review access quarterly with IT on the same cadence as other finance systems.
What do SOC 2 assessors actually ask about close tooling?

Who can change what, whether logs are tamper-evident, and whether evidence of reviews exists. Awards on an About page don’t substitute for immutable who/when/why on recon and journals.
