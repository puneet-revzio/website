AGENT SETUP

Automating journal entries end-to-end

Revzio Editorial · Guide · 8–12 min read

Manual journal construction from exports is one of the largest close bottlenecks. Orion’s Journal Entry Agent generates entries from source data with validations and evidence so your team reviews and posts instead of rebuilding spreadsheets.

Map your chart of accounts

Define how source events map to GL accounts, departments, and entities. Keep a short pilot set (payroll accruals, deferred revenue, bank fees) before expanding. Document materiality thresholds and accounts that must never auto-post.

Encode accrual and recognition logic

Translate existing spreadsheet logic into agent rules: prepaid amortization schedules, commission accruals, and period cutoffs. Pair with the Accruals Agent when schedules are recurring. For revenue-side validation, see the ASC 606 revenue agent guide.

Validations before ERP post

Require balance checks, duplicate detection, and period-open gates. Reviewers approve batches in product; rejected items stay linked to source transactions. This is the same human-in-the-loop model described in human-in-the-loop finance.

Posting and evidence

Once approved, post to your ERP through the integration. Evidence — source extract, calculation, approver — stays attached for audit. Controllers should sample early cycles aggressively, then widen auto-post as confidence grows.

Roll into close cadence

JE prep belongs in the continuous close, not day five. Combine with close workflow checklists and the 3-day close guide so journals are waiting for confirmation at month-end.
